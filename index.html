⚡ Tournament Structure & 4-Block Format

  • Zero Eliminations: Every participant plays all 10 matches (8 Stage 1 mini-pod matches + 2 championship finals matches).
  • Autonomous 6-Player Court Pods: Each court runs independently as a self-contained 6-player pod. Zero inter-court roaming; as soon as a match finishes, resting referees step on as players.
  • Only 3 Reshuffle Moments: Organized reshuffles occur only after Round 3, Round 6 (Halfway Break), and Round 9.
  • "Play 2, Rest 1" Rhythm: Every participant plays exactly 8 matches and officiates 4 matches across Stage 1. Max consecutive plays = 2!
  • 100% Unique Partners: You partner with 8 different teammates across Stage 1—zero partner repeats!

🏸 Match Scoring & Tie-Breakers

  • Stage 1 (Mixer): 1 set to 15 points sudden death (first team to 15 wins; no deuce at 14–14).
  • Leaderboard Ranking Order:
    1. Total Wins
    2. Net Point Differential (+/-)
    3. Total Points Scored
  • Stage 2 Finals: 1 set to 21 points sudden death (or 15 if time constraints apply).

🏆 Stage 2 Finals (4 Courts • Balanced Snake Pairing)

  • Court 1 — Gold Championship: Ranks 1–6 (Balanced Teams: #1 & #6, #2 & #5, #3 & #4)
  • Court 2 — Silver Plate: Ranks 7–12 (Balanced Teams: #7 & #12, #8 & #11, #9 & #10)
  • Court 3 — Bronze Shield: Ranks 13–18 (Balanced Teams: #13 & #18, #14 & #17, #15 & #16)
  • Court 8 — Copper Cup: Ranks 19–24 (Balanced Teams: #19 & #24, #20 & #23, #21 & #22)
  • Round-Robin with Sitting Refs: Each pool plays a 3-match round-robin where the sitting team referees the other two teams (2 plays + 1 ref per player).

👀 Officiating & Diagonal Line Judge Guide

When your team is on rest duty, you and your assigned partner referee that court's match.

  • Ref 1 (Primary Scorer): Announces current score loudly before every serve (e.g. "7 serving 5") and records the final score in this portal.
  • Ref 2 (Line Judge & Shuttle): Manages shuttles and makes decisive sideline/baseline calls.
  • Diagonal Positioning: Stand on opposite diagonal corners for 100% sideline and baseline coverage.
