ℹ️ Dynamic Re-seeding: Final pools are calculated directly from Stage 1 leaderboard ranks (Ranks 1–6 to Gold, 7–12 to Silver, 13–18 to Bronze). Each 3-team pool plays a round-robin (2 matches + 1 ref duty).

⚡ Tournament Structure & Format

  • Zero Eliminations: Every single player plays all 8 matches (6 mixer games in Stage 1 + 2 championship games in Stage 2 Finals).
  • "Play 2, Rest 1" Rhythm: Across 9 Stage 1 rounds, every participant plays 6 matches and officiates 3 matches when resting.
  • 100% Unique Partners: In Stage 1, you partner with 6 different teammates—never the same partner twice!
  • Unbiased Squad Generation: Squads A, B, and C and Round 1 pairings are strictly generated by alphabetical roster order, eliminating any subjective seed bias.

🏸 Match Scoring & Tie-Breakers

  • Stage 1 (Mixer): 1 set to 15 points sudden death (first team to 15 wins; no deuce at 14–14).
  • Leaderboard Ranking Order:
    1. Total Wins
    2. Net Point Differential (+/-)
    3. Total Points Scored
  • Stage 2 Finals: 1 set to 21 points sudden death (or 15 if time constraints apply).

🏆 Stage 2 Finals Qualification

  • Gold Championship (Court 1): Players ranked 1–6 (Teams: #1 & #2, #3 & #4, #5 & #6).
  • Silver Plate (Court 2): Players ranked 7–12 (Teams: #7 & #8, #9 & #10, #11 & #12).
  • Bronze Shield (Court 3): Players ranked 13–18 (Teams: #13 & #14, #15 & #16, #17 & #18).

👀 Officiating & Diagonal Line Judge Guide

When your team is on rest duty, you and your assigned partner referee that court's match.

  • Ref 1 (Primary Scorer): Announces current score loudly before every serve (e.g. "7 serving 5") and records the final score in this portal.
  • Ref 2 (Line Judge & Shuttle): Manages shuttles and makes decisive sideline/baseline calls.
  • Diagonal Positioning: Stand on opposite diagonal corners for 100% sideline and baseline coverage.
